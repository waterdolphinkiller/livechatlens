    Methodology & Workflow

    Four steps from live-chat dispute to bank-ready proof.

    LiveChatLens is a free, open-source (Apache-2.0) US live-chat AI transparency inspector and chargeback evidence toolkit. Rather than guessing AI authorship from prose style, it audits observable interface disclosures, WCAG contrast dark patterns, and merchant commitments — keeping human agent false positives at 0%.

    STEP 01

    Paste or Capture Live

    Paste any support transcript into the Workbench above, or load the Chrome MV3 extension to inspect Salesforce Agentforce, Intercom Fin, Zendesk, Ada, Sierra, Gorgias, Crisp, or Tidio live.

    STEP 02

    Audit Traps & Notices

    Surface store_credit_only eCredit/voucher substitutions, conditional restocking-fee caveats, WCAG low-contrast AI banners, buried ToS footnotes, and human-escalation loops.

    STEP 03

    Match Your Card Issuer

    Select Chase, American Express, Citi, or Capital One to generate a character-limit-safe dispute statement pre-mapped to Visa, Mastercard, or Amex reason codes.

    STEP 04

    Seal & Submit Evidence

    Export a native US Letter PDF for direct bank upload, or download the Sealed Evidence ZIP with SHA-256 manifest, CFPB/CA AB 1609 drafts, and optional RFC 3161 TSA timestamping.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers for US consumers & compliance teams.

    How do I prove an AI chatbot promised me a refund for a US credit card chargeback?+

    Paste your support chat transcript into LiveChatLens (or capture it live with the Chrome extension). LiveChatLens automatically redacts sensitive PII, flags refund or cancellation commitments, maps your case to Visa 13.6, Mastercard 4853, or Amex C02, and exports a bank-ready native PDF and SHA-256 Sealed Evidence ZIP.

    Does LiveChatLens upload my chat transcript or personal information to any server?+

    No. LiveChatLens runs 100% locally in your browser or Node.js with zero cloud uploads and zero tracking. Before exporting any file, it automatically redacts US Social Security Numbers, payment card numbers, bank account numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, OTP codes, and URL authentication tokens.

    What is a store-credit or travel-voucher trap, and why does it matter for disputes?+

    Many US airlines and retailers promise a "full refund" in live chat while quietly limiting it to non-cash store credit, merchandise credit, or future travel eCredit. LiveChatLens tags these as store_credit_only (and flags inspection or restocking-fee deductions as conditional) so your dispute statement clearly documents the substitution.

    How accurate is LiveChatLens, and why doesn't it guess AI from writing style?+

    Probabilistic text detectors frequently misclassify human support agents and non-native English writers. LiveChatLens never guesses from writing style alone — it inspects observable UI disclosures, machine-readable DOM attributes, WCAG contrast, and streaming behavior, abstaining as INCONCLUSIVE when direct evidence is absent to maintain a 0% human false-positive rate.

    Which US credit card dispute portals does the narrative builder support?+

    LiveChatLens includes built-in issuer profiles tailored to Chase (1,000-character limit), American Express (750-character limit), Citi (1,000-character limit), Capital One (800-character limit), and Generic US issuers (1,500 characters), plus a Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA) 60-day written notice reminder.

    What files are inside the Sealed Evidence ZIP, and how can a bank verify it wasn't altered?+

    Each Sealed Evidence ZIP contains chargeback-evidence.pdf, chargeback-report.html, chargeback-narrative.txt, regulatory-complaint-pack.txt (CFPB & California AB 1609 drafts), evidence-receipt.md, receipt.json, optional screenshot.png and timestamp.tsr, and manifest.json with per-file SHA-256 digests verifiable via livechatlens verify or the built-in Receipt Verifier.

    How does optional RFC 3161 timestamping work without exposing my chat privacy?+

    RFC 3161 timestamping is strictly opt-in. When enabled in the Chrome extension or CLI, LiveChatLens sends only the 32-byte SHA-256 hash — never a single word of your chat — to a public Time Stamping Authority (such as FreeTSA) and embeds the signed timestamp.tsr token inside your Sealed ZIP.

    Which live-chat platforms does the Chrome extension inspect?+

    The read-only Chrome MV3 extension detects Salesforce Agentforce (including open Shadow DOM), Intercom Fin, Zendesk Messaging, Ada, Sierra, Gorgias, Crisp, and Tidio, plus a structural heuristic fallback for websites using obfuscated CSS-in-JS class names.

    How can US businesses audit their chatbots for California AB 1609 before January 1, 2027?+

    Engineering and compliance teams can add the LiveChatLens GitHub Action (action.yml) or run node apps/cli/livechatlens.mjs ab1609 <transcript> in CI to automatically fail pull requests if AI disclosure is missing, buried in Terms of Service, low-contrast under WCAG AA, or traps users in a human-escalation loop.

    Is LiveChatLens legal advice or a guarantee that my bank will approve a chargeback?+

    No. LiveChatLens is an observational documentation tool for personal record-keeping, dispute preparation, and QA compliance testing. Final chargeback decisions rest with your card issuer under card-network rules and the US Fair Credit Billing Act.
